# T032 Preregistration — Registered analog-tail retrieval oracle-coverage gate

T032 is the independent second mechanism selected by the external audit. It asks only whether an outer-training-only registered analog library contains tail actions with enough hidden-label coverage to justify selector work.

No legal selector, router, confidence threshold, deployment package, or Kaggle run is authorized. The exact outer-library exclusion, DTW/weight grid, controls, compute cap, and gates are frozen in `config.json`.

Advance only if oracle analog retrieval gains at least 1.0 RMSE over E011, removes at least 15% of E011 shape SSE, helps at least 35% of wells, and is nonnegative in every legacy spatial/typewell group. Otherwise close retrieval immediately.
