# T033 Preregistration — Cross-fitted residual action-manifold capacity gate

## Question

Does the E011 residual population admit a compact, reusable outer-training action space that can preserve sub-5 hidden-label capacity without same-well lookup?

T032 showed that a shuffled residual-action dictionary can outperform registered analog retrieval. T033 separates dictionary/manifold **capacity** from legal identification. Hidden labels are allowed only for held-out oracle fitting or selection. No legal selector, competition prediction, deployment package, or Kaggle execution is authorized.

## Frozen test

Every residual curve is `TVT_true - E011_OOF`, resampled to 128 normalized suffix nodes and bounded at ±160 ft. Across the 25 immutable whole-well contexts, T033 compares outer-training PCA bases, single-centroid dictionaries, two-centroid convex mixtures, deterministic random-source dictionaries, and covariance-matched smooth synthetic dictionaries. Same-well actions are prohibited. Five map actions are averaged before pooled scoring.

## Decision

- **GO:** a compact branch (PCA rank ≤8, single dictionary ≤32, or pair dictionary ≤16) reaches RMSE ≤5.0, removes at least 70% of E011 shape SSE, keeps every legacy group at RMSE ≤6.0, and helps at least 80% of wells.
- **RESEARCH ONLY:** compact RMSE 5.001–6.0.
- **STOP:** no rank ≤16 or dictionary ≤64 branch reaches RMSE ≤6.0.

Passing T033 does not authorize a model. Advancement additionally requires independent T034 evidence that legal observations identify the action manifold.
